# Kennys-Profile

Kehinde Aina is a Bioengineer and Stem Cell Biologist. He commenced his career as an Early-Stage Researcher in the European Consortium for Organ-on-a chip technology with over 7 years of experience. His research focuses on developing Innovative micro-fluidic test platforms for disease modeling, and personalized medicine in line with the 3R principles to replace animal tests where possible; reduce the number of required animals; and refine existing scientific practices while maintaining comparable results. He is currently investigating different organoid models for disease modelling.
